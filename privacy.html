Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what personal information Hasta·Kala collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it plain on purpose.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your mobile number if you add it. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Delivery details: addresses you enter at checkout or save in your profile.
  • Orders: what you bought, from which store, the amount, coupon used, order status and payment status.
  • Activity you create: reviews, wishlist items and messages you send through the contact form.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or net-banking details. Online payments are handled by Razorpay on its own secure pages. We do not run advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To process and deliver your orders and keep your invoices and order history.
  • To show your saved addresses and wishlist back to you.
  • To answer your messages, prevent fraud and keep the service secure.

Who it is shared with

  • The store you order from receives your name, phone number, delivery address and order details so it can fulfil your order.
  • Razorpay processes online payments.
  • Our infrastructure providers (Supabase for the database and sign-in, Vercel for hosting) store and process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your cart. Nothing there is used for advertising. When you pay, Razorpay loads its own checkout, which may set its own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account exists. Order and invoice records are kept as long as needed for accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, phone number and addresses any time in My profile.
  • Delete saved addresses and wishlist items yourself.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, or to close your account, write to . We may need to keep some order records where the law requires.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and access is controlled by account-level permissions. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Hasta·Kala is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
