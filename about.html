About Hasta·Kala

Hasta·Kala is a marketplace that brings local artisan shops online. Every store keeps its own identity, and you can buy handcrafted goods directly from the people who make and sell them.

For shoppers

  • Pick a store, browse its products and add what you like to your cart. One order goes to one store at a time.
  • Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking, or choose cash on delivery.
  • Follow each order from placed to packed, shipped and delivered, and download an invoice.
  • Save addresses and favourite products, and review the things you have received.

For artisan shops

  • A ready online store with products, stock and coupons you control.
  • A counter billing screen for walk-in customers, so online and in-person sales share one inventory.
  • Sales analytics, staff accounts and simple monthly plans.

Want to sell on Hasta·Kala? Add your shop. New shops are reviewed by our team before they go live. Already a shop owner? Sign in here.
