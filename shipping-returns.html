Shipping & Returns

Last updated:

How orders reach you, and what to do if something is not right.

Shipping and delivery

  • Each store packs and ships its own orders. You can follow progress on My Orders: Placed, Packed, Shipped, Delivered.
  • Each store sets its own delivery charge and estimated delivery time, one for addresses near the store and one for the rest of India. Some stores deliver locally only. The charge and estimate are shown on the store and product pages and at checkout, before you pay. Estimates are not guarantees.
  • Some stores offer free delivery above a certain order value.
  • Cash on delivery is offered only by stores that have turned it on; otherwise you pay online.
  • When the store ships your order, it adds the courier name and tracking number, which you can see on My Orders.
  • Please give a complete address and a working mobile number, since the store may call you to deliver.

Cancelling an order

  • Online orders that are not paid within 60 minutes are cancelled automatically.
  • You can cancel an order yourself from My Orders while its status is Placed. Stock and any coupon are returned automatically.
  • Once an order is packed or shipped, contact us with your order number and we will try to stop it.
  • If you had already paid online, the refund is processed as described under Refunds below.

Returns and replacements

You can ask for a return or replacement within days of delivery if the item arrived damaged or defective, or is not what you ordered.

  • Handmade goods vary a little in colour, shape and finish. That is part of their character and not a defect.
  • Items should be unused and in their original packing. Items damaged after delivery by misuse cannot be returned.
  • Made-to-order or personalised items can be returned only if they arrive damaged or incorrect.

How to request a return

  1. Open Contact us and give your order number.
  2. Tell us what is wrong and attach clear photos (include the packing if it was damaged in transit).
  3. We will confirm and tell you how to send the item back, or arrange a replacement.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method within working days of approval.
  • For cash-on-delivery orders, we refund to a bank account or UPI ID you give us.
  • If you paid for an order that had already expired, we refund it in full.
